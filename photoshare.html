Private Photo Sharing – Auto Delete in 24 Hours

Upload your photos securely and receive a unique 5-digit code. Share this code with your friends or send it via WhatsApp. Only the person who enters the code can view your photos. For your privacy, photos will be automatically deleted after 24 hours.

How It Works

  1. Upload – Choose up to 10 photos from your device. No sign-up or registration required.
  2. Get a Code – A unique 5-digit code is generated instantly (e.g., 48291).
  3. Share – Copy the code or send it directly via WhatsApp or any other messenger.
  4. View – Only people with the code can see your photos.
  5. Auto-Delete – Your photos will be permanently removed after 24 hours.

Live Streaming Mode

Prefer not to upload anything? Open the Live tab, pick a folder or files (photos, GIFs, videos or songs) and start the broadcast. You get a 5-digit code just like a normal upload. The viewer enters it in the same View tab, and your media streams directly from your device to theirs. While they watch, you see the same file on your own screen. Keep your tab open while the broadcast is running.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about sharing photos and streaming media with PhotoShare

How does PhotoShare work?
Pick your photos, choose how long they should stay available, and tap Generate Share Code. You instantly get a 5-digit code. Send it to anyone, and they can enter it in the View tab to see your photos. No account, no app download, no hassle.
Do I need to sign up or log in?
Not at all. PhotoShare works instantly as a guest. There is no registration, no email, no phone number and no personal information required, for you or for the people you share with.
How many photos can I share, and what is the size limit?
You can upload up to 10 photos in one share, with each photo up to 15 MB. To keep loading fast for your viewers, photos are automatically resized to a maximum of 1600 pixels before they are uploaded. If you need to share more than that, use Live mode below.
How long do my photos stay available?
You decide. Before generating your code you can set the photos to expire after 10 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 6 hours, 12 hours or 1 day. Once the time is up, the code stops working and the photos are no longer available to anyone. Nothing is kept beyond 24 hours.
Who can see my photos?
Only people who have your 5-digit code. There is no public gallery and no search, so your photos cannot be discovered by strangers. Share the code only with people you trust, and choose a short expiry time if the photos are sensitive.
What is Live mode and how is it different from uploading?
Live mode lets you share a whole folder, or a selection of photos, GIFs, videos and songs, without uploading anything to a server. Your device acts as the host and sends each file directly to your viewer when they open it. It is perfect for large folders, long videos and music that would be too big to upload.
Are my files stored on a server in Live mode?
No. In Live mode your media streams straight from your device to your viewer. Only the 5-digit code is used to connect the two of you. When you stop the broadcast or close the page, the connection ends and nothing is left behind.
Why should I keep my tab open during a live broadcast?
Because your device is the host. If you close the tab, lock the screen for a long time, or lose your internet connection, the stream stops and your viewer will see that the broadcast ended. Keep the page open until your viewer has finished watching. You can start a new broadcast at any time.
Can more than one person watch my live broadcast?
Yes. Anyone who enters your code in the View tab can connect, and the Live Sync Monitor shows how many viewers are connected right now. Each viewer opens files independently, so everyone can watch something different.
Which file formats are supported?
Photos (JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, SVG), videos (MP4, MKV, WEBM, MOV) and audio (MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, AAC). Playback depends on your viewer's browser. MP4 and WEBM work almost everywhere, while MKV is not supported by many browsers and may not play even though it transfers correctly.
My folder has thousands of files. Will it slow things down?
PhotoShare is built for big folders. File lists are shown in batches of 100 with a Show more button, so the page stays smooth for both you and your viewer, even with thousands of files. Your browser may still take a few seconds to read a very large folder when you first select it.
My friend says the code is not working. What should I check?
Make sure the code is exactly 5 digits and has been entered in the View tab. If you shared photos, the code may have expired, so generate a new one with a longer expiry. If you are hosting a live broadcast, check that your tab is still open and that your internet is connected. You can also share the link directly using the Share button, which opens the viewer with the code already filled in.
