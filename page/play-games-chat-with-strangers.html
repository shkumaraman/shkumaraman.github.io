TalkRush Arena – Multiplayer Car Game With Live Chat

TalkRush Arena is a free multiplayer car game that runs right in your browser. You jump in, a match is found, and you are driving in a big arena with real players.

  • Grow Your Car: The more coins you collect, the bigger your car gets.
  • Smash Rivals: Crash into other players to damage them. If a player is destroyed, they drop their coins for others to grab.
  • Nitro and Drift: Use nitro for a speed boost and drift around corners.

It works on both phone and computer. On mobile you get an on-screen joystick and buttons. On desktop you can use WASD or arrow keys, Space for nitro and Shift to drift. No login is needed, so you can start within seconds.
