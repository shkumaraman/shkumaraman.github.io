Featured Verse

Enternal Embers

For in the end, it's not the years that we live,
But the love that we give, and the love that we give.
It's the memories we make, the laughter, and the tears,
That forge a love so strong, and wipe away all our fears.

So let us love, with all our heart, and soul, and mind,
And never let the flame, of our love be left behind.
For love is the answer, to all of life's great quests,
And the key to happiness, and the secret to our rest.

And when the final curtain, of our life's great play,
Falls down, and the lights go out, and our time is gone away,
Let us look back, on the love that we shared,
And know that it was real, and that it was truly rare.

For love is the legacy, that we leave behind,
A gift to the world, and a love that's one of a kind.
It's the flame that burns, the fire that never fades,
A love that's eternal, and forever displayed.

- Nandita
