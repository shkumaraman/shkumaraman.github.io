Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about browsing online users and connecting on TalkRush

How does the live online user list update?
The active list refreshes in real-time. Whenever a stranger joins or interacts on the platform, their presence card appears automatically with an active timestamp. Inactive accounts older than 3 hours are automatically pruned so you only connect with people who are currently online.
Do I need to sign up or log in to message online users?
No registration or personal information is required. You can browse active users instantly as a guest. All you need is a temporary display nickname to send friend requests, exchange messages, and join private chats.
How do I start a private 1v1 chat with someone from the list?
Simply tap the user icon or the add friend button on their profile card. Once connected, tap the message icon to jump straight into your private inbox to share instant text messages, voice notes, and media.
Can I make voice and video calls with active users?
Yes! Once you add someone to your friends list, open their chat thread in your inbox to launch direct browser-based peer-to-peer audio or video calls with no additional plugins or downloads needed.
Is my identity protected while browsing and chatting?
TalkRush is designed around complete anonymity. We do not store sensitive profile credentials, track personal identities, or require phone number linkage. Conversations and calls use secure real-time protocols to keep your discussions private.
What should I do if a user behaves inappropriately?
We maintain automated filters against abusive links and spam. If any user violates community standards, you can remove them from your friends list or use the in-chat report feature to block communication immediately.
