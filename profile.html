Search Users & View Profiles

Welcome to the TalkRush User Profile and Search directory. Looking for someone specific you met in a random chat? Our search tool makes it easy to find friends, view their profiles, and connect instantly using their unique TalkRush ID or nickname.

How to Find Friends Online

Simply enter a user's nickname or ID into the search bar above. Once you find their profile card, you can easily send a friend request or start a direct message. Building your network of friends from previous anonymous chats has never been easier.

  • Easy Search: Look up users instantly by their display name or TR ID.
  • Quick Connect: Add friends with a single click to chat directly later.
  • Manage Connections: Safely unfriend or remove users directly from their profile page.

View a User's Anonymous Posts

Every profile also shows the anonymous posts and confessions shared by that user. Browse a person's public post history, view their photos, and explore more unfiltered posts on the main TalkRush feed.

  • Profile Posts: See every post shared by a specific nickname in one place.
  • Anonymous Confessions: Read a user's public posts, thoughts and shared photos.
  • Explore More: Jump to the full anonymous posts feed anytime.
