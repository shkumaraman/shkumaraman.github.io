Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawaal (FAQs)

TalkRush par chat karne ke bare mein sab kuch jo tumhe janna chahiye

Kya TalkRush bilkul free hai?
Haan, TalkRush 100% free hai! Tumhe koi subscription nahi deni, tokens nahi kharidne, ya credit card nahi add karna. Sabhi random text, video calling, aur group rooms zero cost par available hain.
Interest-based matching kaise kaam karti hai?
Tum header mein interest selector ka use karke apne favorite tags aur interests (jaise Anime, Gaming, Movies, Dating) add kar sakte ho. TalkRush tumhe un strangers ke sath match karne ko priority dega jinke interests tumse milte hain taaki achhi baatcheet ho sake.
Kya main chat ke dauran images aur GIFs bhej sakta hoon?
Bilkul! Tum private 1-on-1 conversations aur public chat rooms dono mein aasani se images, animated GIFs, aur emojis bhej sakte ho. Tum hamare built-in PhotoShare tool ka use karke temporary 24-hour self-destructing image links bhi bhej sakte ho.
Kya personal video aur audio calls sach mein private hain?
Haan. Video aur audio calls direct, encrypted peer-to-peer connections se hoti hain. Hum calls record nahi karte, conversation logs store nahi karte, aur tumhara phone number ya email address nahi maangte, jis se full anonymity milti hai.
Kya main 1v1 chat ke liye private room bana sakta hoon?
Haan! Tum ek unique room code enter karke aasani se ek private room bana sakte ho. Isse tum kisi ko bhi apni real identity bataye bina private 1v1 conversation ke liye invite kar sakte ho.
